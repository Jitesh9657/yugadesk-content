## What Is a URL QR Code?

A URL QR code is a QR code that stores a website address.

When scanned using a phone camera or QR scanner:

1. The QR code is read.
2. The encoded URL is extracted.
3. The website opens automatically.

This eliminates the need for users to manually type web addresses.

## Static vs Dynamic QR Codes

There are two common types of QR codes.

### Static QR Codes

The destination URL cannot be changed after creation.

Best for:

- personal websites
- simple sharing
- permanent links

---

### Dynamic QR Codes

The destination can be updated later.

Often used for:

- marketing campaigns
- business tracking
- frequently changing links

Most simple QR generators create static QR codes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I convert any website into a QR code?

Yes. Most QR generators can create a QR code from any valid website URL.

### Do QR codes expire?

Static QR codes generally do not expire as long as the destination URL remains active.

### Can smartphones scan QR codes without apps?

Most modern smartphones include QR scanning directly in the camera app.

### Are URL QR codes free to create?

Many QR generators allow users to create URL QR codes for free.
