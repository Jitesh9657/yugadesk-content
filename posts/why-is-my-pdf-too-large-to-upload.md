Uploading a PDF should be simple.

However, many people receive messages like:

* "File size exceeds the limit."
* "Upload failed."
* "Maximum file size allowed is 2 MB."

This is a common problem when submitting:

* job applications
* government forms
* college assignments
* business documents

Fortunately, understanding why PDFs become large can help you avoid these issues.

---

## Why PDF Size Matters

Many websites limit upload size to:

* improve performance
* reduce storage requirements
* speed up document processing

If your PDF exceeds the allowed limit, the upload may fail even if the document itself is correct.

---

## Common Reasons PDFs Become Too Large

Several factors can increase PDF size.

### High-Resolution Images

Scanned documents often contain large images. Higher resolution usually means larger file sizes.

---

### Multiple Pages

Adding more pages naturally increases document size. Large reports and presentations often produce bigger PDFs.

---

### Embedded Images

Documents containing photographs usually require more storage than text-only PDFs.

---

### Scanned Documents

Phone cameras and scanners often capture images in very high quality. While this improves readability, it also increases file size.

---

## Why Government Portals Have File Limits

Many government websites request PDF uploads below a specific size.

Examples include:

* examination forms
* identity verification
* tax documents
* application forms

Keeping PDF size within the required limit helps ensure successful uploads.

---

## Job Applications and PDF Size

Employers frequently request:

* resumes
* cover letters
* certificates

in PDF format.

Combining unnecessary high-resolution images can create oversized documents that become difficult to upload.

## Image vs PDF

Images are useful for viewing.

PDFs are better for:

* printing
* document sharing
* applications
* official records

PDF also preserves formatting across different devices.

---

## Common Mistakes That Increase PDF Size

Many users accidentally:

* scan documents at unnecessarily high resolution
* include large photographs
* duplicate pages
* convert already oversized images into PDFs

Reviewing documents before uploading can prevent many of these problems.

---

## Best Practices for Smaller PDFs

To keep PDF files manageable:

* use clear but reasonable image resolution
* avoid unnecessary pages
* organize documents before creating the PDF
* optimize images before conversion
* review the final document before uploading

These practices improve compatibility with most online portals.
