## What Does It Mean to Merge PDF Files?

Merging PDFs means combining two or more PDF documents into a single file.

For example:

Instead of sharing:

- Resume.pdf
- CoverLetter.pdf
- Certificates.pdf

you can combine them into:

```txt
Job-Application.pdf
```

This creates a cleaner and more professional document package.

## PDF Merge vs PDF Split

These are opposite operations.

### Merge PDF

Combines multiple files into one.

---

### Split PDF

Separates one PDF into multiple files.

## Does Merging Affect PDF Quality?

- layout

However, results may vary depending on the files being merged.
